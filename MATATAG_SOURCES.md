# MATATAG curriculum sources used by this starter

This starter is structured from the Department of Education MATATAG curriculum
guides dated August 2023:

1. **MATATAG Curriculum: Reading and Literacy (Grade 1)**
2. **MATATAG Curriculum: English (Grades 2-10)**

Official DepEd learning-resource portals list both curriculum guides under the
Revised/Enhanced K to 10 MATATAG resources.

Important curriculum interpretation for this application:

- Grade 1: Language + Reading and Literacy are the foundational L1 learning
  areas. An English-reading product should label generated English material as
  an adaptation of those foundational competencies rather than as an official
  Grade 1 English subject competency.
- Grades 2-3: English is a MATATAG learning area, so the service uses `EN2...`
  and `EN3...` competency codes.

`curriculum.py` is a curated reading-focused registry, not a replacement for the
complete official curriculum guides. When updating or expanding it, verify codes,
quarters, subdomains, and wording against the latest official DepEd material.
